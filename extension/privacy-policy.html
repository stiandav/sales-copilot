Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Cold Call AI Assistant ("we", "our", "the extension") is a Chrome extension that provides real-time sales coaching for real estate cold calls. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

What Data We Collect

  • Account information: Email address and authentication credentials when you create an account.
  • Subscription data: Payment status and plan type (processed by Stripe; we do not store credit card numbers).
  • Agent profile: Name, team, market area, and value proposition — stored locally on your device.
  • Custom scripts: Any scripts you upload or paste — stored locally on your device.
  • Usage preferences: Lead type selection, listen mode, API keys — stored locally on your device.

Audio and Microphone Data

  • Mic mode: Uses the Web Speech API built into Chrome. Audio is processed locally by your browser. No audio is sent to our servers.
  • Tab Audio mode (Pro): Audio is sent directly from your browser to Deepgram's speech-to-text service using your own API key. We do not relay, store, or have access to this audio.
  • Voice Practice: Uses your browser's built-in speech recognition and text-to-speech. No audio leaves your device.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not record or store any call audio.
  • We do not track the content of your conversations.
  • We do not collect browsing history or activity outside the extension.
  • We do not sell, share, or trade any personal data with third parties.
  • We do not use any data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage

  • Local storage: Scripts, agent profile, preferences, and session data are stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API.
  • Cloud storage: Only your email address and subscription status are stored in our database (Supabase) for authentication and billing purposes.
  • Payment processing: All payment processing is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card information.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Authentication and subscription management. Privacy policy
  • Stripe: Payment processing (only if you subscribe). Privacy policy
  • Deepgram: Speech-to-text (only if you use Tab Audio mode with your own API key). Privacy policy
  • Google: OAuth sign-in (only if you choose Google sign-in). Privacy policy

Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your data from our servers within 30 days. Local data can be cleared at any time by removing the extension.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data by viewing your profile in the extension.
  • Delete your local data by removing the extension from Chrome.
  • Request deletion of your account and cloud data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact
